## The eight hardest questions

Answers live in `docs/12-judge-qa.md` and are written there once, not twice. Listed here so the
presenter knows what is coming.

1. What is the actual algorithm, and why is that the right model for this problem?
2. What is real right now and what is stubbed?
3. Where does the data come from, and what happens at ten times the volume?
4. Why would this not be a feature inside an existing product in six months?
5. Who pays, how much, and how did you get to that number?
6. What regulation applies, and what would stop you shipping this?
7. What does one inference cost you, and what happens to that at scale?
8. What did you cut, and why?

## Delivery rules

- One presenter, one backup, both named in `docs/10-demo-script.md`.
- Plain words. No feature names nobody has heard. "Camera" beats "photo plus AI".
- Say the synthetic-data sentence every time, unprompted.
- Never say "we did not have time". Say what we cut and why, which is a judgment story.
