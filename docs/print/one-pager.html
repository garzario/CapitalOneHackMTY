Why the window matters

Fiscal risk plus irreversible payment

A new definitive Article 69-B publication can change the fiscal effect of invoices already paid. Once the wrong SPEI leaves, the product can only explain the loss. Ceptinela joins the evidence while the clerk can still hold, verify or release.

What a judge can verify

  • Type a real RFC into the read-only SAT lookup. Real RFCs are never paired with fabricated evidence.
  • Open each detector beside its test in packages/core and run the suite offline.
  • Once the real one-cent CEP lands, inspect its signed XML, holder comparison and beneficiary registry.
  • Trace every decision through the append-only LedgerEvent contract.

Six controls, one human decision

  1. SAT 69-B plus replayMatch current status, then quantify prior exposure when the list changes.
  2. CLABE forensicsCheck digit, institution, plaza, account history and OCR-aware edit distance.
  3. Duplicate invoicesDetect UUID, folio and near-duplicate collisions without joining different issuers.
  4. Supplier behaviorCompare cadence, amount and concentration with the supplier's own valid history.
  5. CEP beneficiaryVerify signed evidence and compare the credited holder with the CFDI legal name.
  6. Expected lossWeigh amount at risk against delay cost, then ask a person to decide.
